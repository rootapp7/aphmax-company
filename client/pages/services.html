How supply works

From requirement to delivery.

APHMAX uses a demand-driven merchandise supply model designed around client specifications, order volumes and timelines.

01

Understand the requirement

Confirm items, specifications, quantities, delivery location and timing against the client’s stated needs.

02

Identify and vet suppliers

Supplier identification and vetting support dependable sourcing, competitive pricing and suitable products.

03

Coordinate the order

Order placement and coordination make each order traceable and accountable from sourcing through fulfilment.

04

Verify before dispatch

Quality verification and pre-delivery checks help confirm items meet agreed specifications.

05

Deliver and confirm

Delivery coordination, documentation and client acknowledgement complete the supply cycle.

Flexible supply for different operating needs

Services can support routine replenishment, special projects, events, bulk procurement and phased deliveries. Specialized handling can be arranged for sensitive or high-value items according to order requirements.
