Health, safety and environment

HSE built into every operation.

The company is fully committed to integrating Health, Safety and Environmental considerations into all its operations, for the wellbeing of employees, clients, partners and the surrounding community, while minimizing environmental impact.

HSE practice areas

Protect people, goods and the environment.

APHMAX’s HSE commitment covers workplace health, safe operations, merchandise handling, risk control, emergency readiness and environmental responsibility.

Health protection

Promote clean and hygienic workplaces, identify potential health risks and provide suitable protective equipment where necessary.

Workplace safety

Apply safe work practices, safety awareness and emergency procedures while observing applicable occupational safety requirements.

Safe goods handling

Use appropriate packaging, labelling, storage and transport practices to reduce damage, contamination and accidents.

Risk assessment

Identify operational hazards and apply controls such as procedures, safety signage and preventive maintenance.

Emergency preparedness

Maintain response procedures for incidents such as fire, accidents or environmental spills.

Environmental responsibility

Support responsible sourcing, proper waste disposal, recycling or reuse where applicable and compliance with environmental requirements.

Review and improve

The profile identifies staff sensitisation, training and ongoing review of HSE practices as part of continuous improvement.
