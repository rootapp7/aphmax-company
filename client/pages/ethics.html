Ethics and compliance register10 principles
A

Lawful and fair procurement

A1

Compliance with Government Procurement Laws

The company follows all applicable government procurement laws, regulations and guidelines, so tendering and contracting processes are lawful, fair and transparent.

A2

Conflict of Interest Management

The company supports fair and open competition and refrains from practices that may distort or undermine competitive procurement processes.

A3

Fair Competition Practices

Fair and open competition is supported, and practices that could distort competitive procurement are avoided.

B

Honest business conduct

B1

Ethical Business Conduct

All business activities are conducted with honesty, integrity and professionalism, with ethical decision-making in all dealings with clients, suppliers and partners.

B2

Transparency and Accountability

Business transactions are properly documented and traceable, keeping financial and operational activities open and accountable.

B3

Confidentiality and Data Protection

Sensitive and confidential information is safeguarded and used strictly for legitimate business purposes, in line with data protection requirements.

C

Oversight and response

C1

Internal Controls and Oversight

Internal control systems monitor compliance, prevent irregularities and ensure responsible management of resources.

C2

Reporting of Unethical Conduct

Employees and stakeholders are encouraged to report suspected unethical or non-compliant behavior through established internal reporting channels.

C3

Corrective and Disciplinary Measures

Any breach of ethical or compliance standards is investigated, and corrective or disciplinary action is taken to prevent recurrence.
